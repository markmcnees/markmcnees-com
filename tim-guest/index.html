What to send me before we record

This helps me introduce you well and lets us promote you, your business, FSU Entrepreneurship and Innovation, and the Jim Moran College and Institute.

Your name

Founder or founders, or your name if you’re faculty or staff.

Your FSU connection

Graduation year and major, or your position, or your JMI connection.

A short description

What your business does, or what you do for FSU, JMC, or JMI. A few sentences is plenty.

Website and social links

Every address you’d like us to tag or share.

Your logo

If you have one. A PNG with a transparent background works best.

Email your details to Mark at mmcnees@fsu.edu

How to find the studio

The studio is on the third floor of the William Johnston Building (WJB), next to the College of Fine Arts and Landis Green. Campus map and directions to WJB.

  1. Take the elevator to the third floor. Check that the room numbers start with 3.
  2. Turn right out of the elevator.
  3. Walk past the Office Depot Technology Complex.
  4. The studio is the last door on the right, at the end of the hall.

How the conversation goes

No script and no prep homework. I want to get to know you and hear your story. Here’s the order we usually follow.

  1. The intro

    I open the show.

  2. Welcome

    I introduce you.

  3. Where you started

    How you grew up, and who, if anyone, showed you what entrepreneurship looks like.

  4. FSU

    Your time at FSU or your connection to it.

  5. Your business

    What you built and how it works.

  6. The hard parts

    The real challenges of being an entrepreneur.

Every guest gets the same last question

“If you could go back in time and were able to tell yourself one thing before you started your business, what would you tell yourself?”

It’s the one question worth thinking about ahead of time.
